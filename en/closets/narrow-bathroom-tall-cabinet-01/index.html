A narrow, tall cabinet in a bathroom corner, with a white door in an aluminium frame and shelves for cleaning products and towels.

A narrow, tall cabinet built for a bathroom corner between the wall and the shower. The white door is framed in an aluminium profile and opens along the full height. Inside, white shelves hold cleaning products, baskets and towels, with space for taller items at the bottom. A corner that was not used became tidy storage. Designed and built at Shalito carpentry to the size of the corner.
