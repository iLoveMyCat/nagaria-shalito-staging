Wall closets at Shalito Carpentry

A good wall closet uses the whole wall, floor to ceiling, and fits the room rather than the other way round. In our Ashdod workshop we build bedroom closets, niche closets, corner closets and tall narrow cabinets for bathrooms.

On this page you will find white floor-to-ceiling closets with metal handles, mirrored sliding doors, walnut combined with white doors, a matt light closet with aluminium profile handles, and cream framed fronts with brass knobs. Inside we fit open shelves, angled shoe shelves, drawers or a glass-shelf display unit.

Sliding doors suit a narrow room because they need no space to open. Hinged doors open the whole closet at once. We help you choose by room size and what you will store.

We measure at your home, check walls, ceiling and sockets, and plan the inside with you. The closet is built in the workshop and installed by us.

It suits rooms with uneven walls, unusual ceiling heights or an air conditioner in the way, and children's rooms whose needs change over the years.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a wall closet cost?

We set the price after measuring, because it depends on the size, the material and finish, the hardware and the installation. Measuring and the quote are free. You can send us photos and rough measurements on WhatsApp first, so we understand the job before we meet.

Sliding or hinged doors?

In a narrow room, or when the bed is close to the closet, sliding doors save space. Hinged doors give access to the whole closet at once. A mirror can go on either. We recommend after seeing the room.

Can you build up to the ceiling or into a niche?

Yes. We have built floor-to-ceiling and niche closets, and you can see them on this page. When measuring we check whether walls and ceiling are straight and fit the closet to them.
