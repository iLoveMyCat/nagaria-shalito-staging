Long stepped TV unit with an electric fireplace

A long, low light-coloured unit on two levels along a green wall, with an electric fireplace built in under the TV.

A long, low unit in a light tone running the full length of the living-room wall. The part under the TV is taller and holds a built-in electric fireplace; the rest steps down to a lower level towards the corner. The fronts are plain and handleless, so the line stays calm. The light tone stands out against the green wall and next to the wood-slat cladding by the window. Designed and built at Shalito carpentry to the length of the wall.
