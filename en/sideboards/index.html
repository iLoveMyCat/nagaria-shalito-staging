Sideboards and TV units at Shalito Carpentry

A sideboard or TV wall is the first thing you see in the living room. Built to measure, it sits exactly on the wall, the cables are hidden and every device has its place.

We have built wall-hung sideboards, units on metal or slim legs, a long two-level sideboard and units with a built-in electric fireplace under the TV. In some homes we also clad the TV wall, for example in dark walnut with a recessed frame for the screen. There is also a stepped bedroom chest on this page.

Finishes on this page: handleless white gloss, light grey with a dark top, oak with sage green, greyish wood. Inside we plan wide drawers, doors, open niches and a tidy route for cables.

Sometimes it starts with a hand sketch. We measure the wall, check the sockets and TV point, and plan height and length. A wall-hung unit needs a suitable wall, so we check it in advance.

It suits a long living-room wall, a wall with a window or column, and homes with many devices. If you plan to change the TV soon, tell us the new screen size.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a sideboard cost?

We set the price after measuring, because it depends on the size, the material and finish, the hardware and the installation. Measuring and the quote are free. You can send us photos and rough measurements on WhatsApp first, so we understand the job before we meet.

Can a floating unit go on any wall?

Usually yes, but it depends on the wall type and the weight. A plasterboard wall, for example, needs reinforcement. We check it when measuring.

Can you build in an electric fireplace?

Yes, we have built several. Choose the fireplace before planning so we build its opening to the exact size.

How are the cables hidden?

We plan cable routes in the back and shelves in advance, and check where sockets and the TV point are when measuring, so cables stay out of sight.
