What makes up the price

Everything we make is built to measure, so the price is set after measuring. Here is what affects it.

Size. The longer and taller the piece, the more material and work it needs. Kitchens are measured by cabinet length, closets by wall width and height.

Material and finish. The type of front and its finish change the price more than anything else. TODO: which materials you offer and how they affect the price.

Hardware and inside fittings. Hinges, drawer runners, pull-out baskets, corner mechanisms and roller shutters add convenience, and cost.
