Bathroom vanities at Shalito Carpentry

A bathroom is usually small with awkward dimensions: fixed plumbing, a washing machine, a window or a corner. A made-to-measure vanity makes full use of that space.

We have built wall-hung vanities with drawers, units with open towel shelves, tall storage columns, a cabinet over the toilet and one next to the washing machine. You will see white fronts with a dark wood top, a green vanity with a black top and an old-rose basin, a natural wood vanity with a black basin and tap, and a rounded triangular basin. We measure at home, including pipes and drain, then design, build and install. A wall-hung vanity keeps the floor clear and easy to clean; a standing one gives more storage.

It suits small bathrooms, guest toilets that need a shallow cabinet, and anyone who wants the vanity to match the rest of the home. If you are renovating, talk to us before the plumber fixes the pipe positions.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a bathroom vanity cost?

We set the price after measuring, because it depends on the size, the material and finish, the hardware and the installation. Measuring and the quote are free. You can send us photos and rough measurements on WhatsApp first, so we understand the job before we meet.

Wall-hung or standing?

A wall-hung vanity looks lighter and keeps the floor free for cleaning. A standing one holds more and suits walls that are not good for hanging. We recommend after seeing the room.
