Walnut-look walk-in closet in Ramla

An open walnut-look walk-in closet in Ramla with full-height shelves, hanging sections and drawers with black handles, from empty room to finish.

A walk-in closet in Ramla, documented from the empty room to the finish. We built an open walk-in in a dark walnut-look finish along the wall. One side has closely spaced full-height shelves for folded clothes and shoes; the other has hanging sections with drawers below. The drawers have black handles, and lighting is built into some shelves. The photos also show the assembly on site. Designed, built and installed by Shalito carpentry.
