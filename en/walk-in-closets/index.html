Walk-in closets at Shalito Carpentry

A walk-in closet keeps clothes, shoes and bags in one place and frees the bedroom from cabinets. Even a small or narrow room can work well if it is planned properly.

Here you will find open walk-ins we built in oak, dark wood and walnut tones, all with full-height shelves, hanging space and drawers. In one we fitted pull-down hanging rails so the top of the wall can be used without a ladder. In a narrow one we planned double-height hanging and a low chest with a top.

Planning starts with what you own: how many long and short items, how many pairs of shoes, who uses the room. The walls are then divided between hanging, shelves and drawers. The room can be fully open or have doors. TODO: whether you also fit lighting and mirrors.

After measuring you get a design and a quote. We build every part in the workshop and assemble it at your home. For some projects we photographed the whole way, from drawing or empty room to the finished closet.

It suits couples who share the space, families who have outgrown a standard closet, and anyone building a house who wants a dedicated dressing room.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a walk-in closet cost?

We set the price after measuring, because it depends on the size, the material and finish, the hardware and the installation. Measuring and the quote are free. You can send us photos and rough measurements on WhatsApp first, so we understand the job before we meet.

How small can a walk-in closet be?

There is no single answer. We have built narrow walk-ins too, as long as there is room to stand and open drawers comfortably. TODO: minimum width you recommend. We check it when measuring.

Open or with doors?

An open room is quick to use and you see everything. Doors keep dust off and look tidier. You can also mix: part open, part closed.

Can you turn an existing room into a walk-in?

Yes. We measure the room as it is, including the window, sockets and air conditioner, and plan around them.
