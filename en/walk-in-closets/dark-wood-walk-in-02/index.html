Dark wood-look walk-in closet

Walk-in closet · 21 photos

Draft text, awaiting approval
All 21 photos

A narrow dark wood-look walk-in with two-level hanging, a low cabinet with a top and full-height shelves, from drawing to installation.

The project starts with a hand drawing and carcasses built in the workshop, moves to the narrow room before the change, with clothes piled on basic shelves, and ends with an orderly walk-in in a dark wood-look finish. The short wall has two levels of hanging, and along the long wall a low cabinet with a top sits below a tall unit. We also added full-height open shelves and narrow shoe shelves. Designed, built and installed by Shalito carpentry.
