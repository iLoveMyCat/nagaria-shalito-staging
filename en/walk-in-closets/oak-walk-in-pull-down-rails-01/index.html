Oak-look walk-in closet with pull-down rails

Walk-in closet · 23 photos

Draft text, awaiting approval
All 23 photos

An open oak-look walk-in closet with pull-down hanging rails, full-height shelves and drawers, from the workshop to installation.

The project is documented from the start: cutting and drilling panels in the workshop, the empty room, assembly on site and the result. The walk-in is open, oak-look, and runs along two walls. One side has two levels of hanging with drawers below; the other has full-height shelves for folded clothes. In the upper hanging areas we fitted pull-down rails with a handle, so high clothes are easy to reach. Designed, built and installed by Shalito carpentry.
