A family workshop in Ashdod

We are a family carpentry workshop in Ashdod, and for more than 30 years we have been building made-to-measure furniture for homes in the area: kitchens, wall closets, walk-in closets, sideboards, bathroom vanities and studies. We measure in your home, design, build in our own workshop and install.

How it works

  1. Meeting and measuring

    We come to your home, hear what you need and measure the space.

  2. Design

    Together we plan the layout, materials and finish, and you get a quote.

  3. Built in our workshop

    Once you approve, the furniture is built in our workshop in Ashdod.

  4. Installation

    We deliver and install it, and check with you that everything works.
