Light grey kitchen with a lit display cabinet

Kitchen · 7 photos

Draft text, awaiting approval
All 7 photos

An L-shaped kitchen with handleless light grey fronts, a glass display cabinet with blue lighting, a tall oven unit and a fridge niche.

An L-shaped kitchen with light grey fronts and a grip profile instead of handles. Along the wall there are drawers and base cabinets, a light countertop and upper cabinets. On one side stands a tall white unit with a built-in oven. Next to the fridge we built a display cabinet with glass shelves and blue LED lighting, with closed cupboards below. The display cabinet separates the dining corner from the kitchen and adds light in the evening.
