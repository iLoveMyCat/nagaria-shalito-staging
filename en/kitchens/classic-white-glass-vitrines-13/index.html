Classic white kitchen with glass cabinets

Kitchen · 18 photos

A classic kitchen with white framed fronts, tall glass cabinets with divided panes, a tall oven unit and a grey countertop.

A classic-style kitchen with white framed fronts and metal handles. It runs straight along one wall: at one end a tall unit with a built-in oven and a place for a microwave, in the middle base cabinets with a grey countertop and upper cabinets, some with glass doors. At the other end stands a tall display cabinet with divided glass doors, next to a fridge niche. The backsplash differs between photos because the kitchen was photographed at different stages.
