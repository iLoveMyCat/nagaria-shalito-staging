Kitchens at Shalito Carpentry

A kitchen is the room you work in most, so we plan it around your home, not a catalogue. In our Ashdod workshop we have built straight, L-shaped and U-shaped kitchens, kitchens with an island or a breakfast bar, and small kitchenettes, one of them hidden behind folding doors.

Fronts change from project to project: handleless white gloss, colours such as cream, cappuccino, champagne, green and purple, wood-look fronts, and classic or country kitchens with framed doors and glazed cabinets. Many clients chose printed glass for the backsplash or an upper door. Materials we work with: TODO: which of polymer, oven paint, laminate, veneer you offer.

What is inside the cabinets matters just as much: pull-out corner baskets, internal drawers, a pantry with baskets, an appliance cabinet with a roller shutter, a hood hidden in an upper cabinet. We choose these together when planning.

It starts with measuring in your home, then the design and quote. Once you approve, the kitchen is built in our workshop and installed at your place.

It suits people moving into a new home, families replacing an old kitchen, and small flats where every cabinet has to work hard. Bring photos of kitchens you like to the meeting.

A ready-made kitchen, from IKEA for example, is usually cheaper and quicker, and sometimes that is enough. A workshop kitchen is built to your walls, pipes and appliances, so no space is wasted. We work in Ashdod and the area: TODO: towns you serve.

Why not buy a ready-made kitchen?

A ready-made kitchen is a good option when your room fits its modules. When there is an uneven wall, a column, awkward plumbing, or you want storage up to the ceiling, a made-to-measure kitchen solves it. We also install it ourselves.
