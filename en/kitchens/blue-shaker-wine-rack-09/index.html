Blue-grey shaker kitchen with wooden glass cabinets

Kitchen · 20 photos

Draft text, awaiting approval
All 20 photos

A large L-shaped kitchen with blue-grey framed fronts, wood-framed glass wall cabinets, wine bottle cubbies and a breakfast bar with a wooden top.

Shalito carpentry designed and built this large country-style L-shaped kitchen. The fronts are blue-grey with a frame around every door and black handles, and the wall cabinets have glass doors in light wood frames. The countertop is light grey, with a patterned tile backsplash. The end of the run has open cubbies for wine bottles, and around the corner is a breakfast bar with a wooden top and a small wall cabinet. On the other side are an oven tower, tall units and a black fridge. Some photos were taken during installation.
