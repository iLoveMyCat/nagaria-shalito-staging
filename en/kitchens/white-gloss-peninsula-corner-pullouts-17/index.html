White kitchen with island and corner pull-outs

Kitchen · 24 photos

Draft text, awaiting approval
All 24 photos

A handleless white gloss kitchen with a dark grey countertop, an island, pull-out corner baskets, inner drawers and a roller-shutter appliance cabinet.

A large kitchen with white gloss fronts, a grip profile instead of handles and a dark grey countertop. Along the walls there are base and upper cabinets, a tall unit with a built-in oven, a fridge niche and a roller-shutter appliance cabinet. Facing the kitchen is an island with storage on both sides. The corners have pull-out basket mechanisms, and the cabinets hold inner drawers, narrow bottle baskets and a place for the dishwasher. Some photos show our team installing the cooker hood.
