Dark kitchen during installation

Kitchen · 8 photos · During installation

Draft text, awaiting approval
All 8 photos

An L-shaped kitchen with dark fronts and profile handles, wall cabinets combining dark and white, and a tall unit wall, before the countertop was fitted.

These photos record the kitchen during installation, before the final countertop and backsplash were fitted. Shalito carpentry built and installed the cabinets. The base units form an L, with dark fronts and horizontal aluminium profile handles. By the window stands a wall of dark tall units with an open niche, and the end of the run has a unit with open shelves. The wall cabinets combine dark and white fronts. The last photo shows the sink opening before fitting.
