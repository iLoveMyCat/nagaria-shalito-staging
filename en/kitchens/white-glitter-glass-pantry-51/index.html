White kitchen with glitter glass and pantry

Kitchen · 5 photos

Draft text, awaiting approval
All 5 photos

An L-shaped kitchen with glossy white fronts and profile handles, a glitter glass wall cabinet, a light countertop and a tall pantry with pull-out baskets.

Shalito carpentry designed and built this glossy white L-shaped kitchen with profile handles and wide drawers. Above the sink are white wall cabinets, with a glitter glass front along their lower row. On the other side is a wall of tall units with an open niche and a pantry that opens onto pull-out baskets and shelves. The countertop is light and speckled, and a small counter stands at the front. The photos were taken when installation was finished, before the kitchen was in use.
