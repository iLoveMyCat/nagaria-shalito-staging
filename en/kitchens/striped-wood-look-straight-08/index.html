Straight kitchen with striped wood-look fronts

Kitchen · 3 photos

Draft text, awaiting approval
All 3 photos

A straight-run kitchen with dark striped wood-look fronts and profile handles, a light countertop and a printed glass wall cabinet.

Shalito carpentry designed and built this straight-run kitchen with dark striped wood-look base fronts and aluminium profile handles. The light countertop holds an integrated sink and a gas hob with a chimney hood above. Instead of a full row of wall cabinets there is one cabinet with a glass front printed with waves in brown and white. At the end of the run, light tall units surround the fridge and the built-in oven.
