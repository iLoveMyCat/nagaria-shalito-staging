White kitchen with island and orchid glass

Kitchen · 16 photos

Draft text, awaiting approval
All 16 photos

A white kitchen with an island, a light countertop, an orchid printed glass upper cabinet, a pantry with baskets and a roller-shutter appliance cabinet.

At Shalito Carpentry we designed and built a white kitchen with profile handles and a light countertop. An island with storage stands in the middle of the room, and above the sink, next to the hood, an upper cabinet has a printed glass front with orchids. The tall cabinet wall holds a fridge niche, a pantry with pull-out baskets, a built-in oven and microwave, and a roller-shutter appliance cabinet. The corner cabinet has a pull-out basket mechanism, and the upper cabinets open upwards on lift mechanisms.
