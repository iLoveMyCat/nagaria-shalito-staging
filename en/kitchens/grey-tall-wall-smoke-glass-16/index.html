Kitchen with island and tall cabinet wall

Kitchen · 5 photos

Draft text, awaiting approval
All 5 photos

A kitchen with a wall of tall grey cabinets, a black-fronted fridge, an island with a white top and a purple-swirl printed glass cabinet.

At Shalito Carpentry we designed and built this kitchen around a wall of tall grey cabinets with a black-fronted double-door fridge, a built-in oven and a microwave. Facing it is an island with a white top and seating, with a narrow pull-out basket unit at its end. Along the other wall run cream base cabinets with drawers and a hob, and an upper cabinet with a printed glass front in a purple swirl. A stainless steel hood hangs above the hob.
