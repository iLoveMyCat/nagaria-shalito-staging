Champagne gloss kitchen with breakfast bar

Kitchen · 9 photos

Draft text, awaiting approval
All 9 photos

A kitchen with champagne gloss fronts and profile handles, a breakfast bar with a grey top, marble-look walls and dark wood slats.

At Shalito Carpentry we designed and built a kitchen with champagne gloss fronts and profile handles along the drawers. At the entrance stands a breakfast bar with a grey top, beside a panel of vertical dark wood slats that separates the kitchen from the rest of the home. Along the wall there are drawers, a hob and a stainless steel hood, and in the corner a roller-shutter appliance cabinet. The walls are clad in large grey marble-look tiles.
