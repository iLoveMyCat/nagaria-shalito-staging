Accessibility statement

Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to use this site, including people with disabilities. The site was built according to the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013, and Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.1.

Level of compliance

The site was built to conform with level AA of IS 5568 and WCAG 2.1, except for the limitations listed below.

What we did

Clear structure: headings in order, marked navigation regions and a defined language on every page (Hebrew right-to-left, English and Russian left-to-right).

Keyboard: every link, button and form field can be reached with the keyboard, with a clearly visible focus indicator and a skip-to-content link at the top of each page.

Colour contrast: text and buttons meet the AA contrast ratio.

Images: every image has alternative text describing what it shows. Decorative images are marked so screen readers skip them.

Forms: every field has a label, and error messages are written in words and linked to the right field.

Zoom: text can be enlarged to 200% without losing content, and the layout adapts to narrow screens without sideways scrolling.

Motion: if you have turned on reduced motion on your device, the site's animations stop.

We did not install an accessibility overlay widget. Accessibility is built into the site's own code, and you can use your browser's and operating system's tools.

Known limitations

The alternative texts of project photos are a first draft, and we are improving them gradually.

Some older photos are low quality, so small details are hard to see.
