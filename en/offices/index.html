Offices and studies at Shalito Carpentry

A good workspace needs room for a computer, paperwork and books without taking over the room. We build made-to-measure furniture for offices and home studies.

Projects here include a large L-shaped executive desk with a curved front in glossy wood with black edging and drawer units on castors; a home study with a floor-to-ceiling oak bookcase and matching desk; a warm wood study with glass-door upper cabinets and a glass-top desk; a built-in white work corner with red edging; and a child's room unit with a desk, drawers and shelves.

We plan for the person who sits there every day: desk height, legroom, cable routes, sockets and what needs to be within reach. Often the desk and a bookcase are combined on one wall as a single unit.

It suits people working from home, children who need a homework desk, and offices that want a desk, bookcase or cabinets fitted to the room. We also plan for light, so a window does not glare on the screen.
